# INC-2026-09-11 — a customer's ledger shows a spend they did not make

**Raised:** 11 Sep 2026, 03:14 IST
**Severity:** S2 — wrong financial data shown to a user
**Status:** OPEN, assigned to you

---

## What happened

Overnight support picked up a complaint from a user on account ending **4821.

> "Your app says I spent ₹92,213.10 on a water can. I paid ₹5. Fix this, I am
> not able to trust anything else on this screen now."

The Track screen does show a debit of **₹92,213.10** against merchant
**UPI/WATER CAN**. The user is right: the bank SMS behind it says ₹5.

## The alert that fired

```
2026-09-11T03:14:06+05:30  ALERT  ledger.balance.divergence
  account=**4821
  ledger_derived_balance=-1213003.85
  bank_stated_balance=41126.34
  divergence=-1254130.19
  window=last_45d
```
